LineFour

The hook is already written. The four bars in front of it are stuck because both hands move the same way. LineFour puts a live FluidR3 violin against an upright bass so one line climbs while the other falls. Not a third stack. Not a walking chorus. Not a louder master.

Distinct from ThirdFour, WalkFour, EarnFour, ShakeFour.

First play loads FluidR3 violin, upright bass, piano, and woodblock. Flat note names only. Sharps are pitched from the nearest live sample.

Bars walk I–vi–IV–V. Contrary: violin descends while bass rises into the hook. Same-way: both sit on roots, which is why the loop never arrives.
